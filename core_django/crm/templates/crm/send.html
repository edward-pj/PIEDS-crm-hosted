Send queues the mail, it does not send it. You land on Scheduled sends next — press Send queued mail now there to send it from your Gmail. There is no time-of-day restriction; any hour works.

, and every row box rendered `checked`. Mailing two people therefore meant finding a collapsed section and un-ticking several hundred boxes, and the obvious action mailed the entire assigned list. Boxes now start empty, and picking two is two clicks. "Send all" stays a single click because that is what it is for: it posts one flag and lets the server re-resolve the queue, so it works at any size and does not care what the table shows or how many boxes are ticked.
